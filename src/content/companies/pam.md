PAM is a platform used by UK parent-teacher associations (PTAs) to run events, sell tickets, hold prize draws and auctions, fill volunteer rotas, send newsletters, keep accounts and claim Gift Aid.

PAM is written in Elixir. It uses Phoenix LiveView for its web interface, the Ash framework for its domain model and Oban for background jobs.
