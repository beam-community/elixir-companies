Erlnets Technologies designs and builds high-availability backend systems, most of them in healthcare. Its main work is the architecture and development, in Elixir and OTP, of a multi-tenant telemedicine and remote monitoring platform that has run in production since 2023 and manages 10,000 hubs and 30,000 medical devices.

It also publishes open source libraries for distributed systems on the BEAM, among them Malla, an Elixir framework for distributed services composed from plugins, and NkSIP, a SIP toolkit in Erlang.
