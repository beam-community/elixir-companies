Treat Health is building healthcare where the right care takes minutes, not days. Patients describe their concern in their own words, our smart intake asks the follow-up questions a doctor would ask, and a licensed doctor decides on the care. The patient and doctor services run on Elixir and Phoenix LiveView, alongside React and native mobile apps.
