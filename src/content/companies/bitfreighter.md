Bitfreighter is an EDI and API integration platform for freight brokers, 3PLs, and carriers. Its products include ShipperConnect for EDI and API shipper integrations and LiveQuote for automated spot quoting and real-time rating.

Bitfreighter uses Elixir for nearly all of its backend programming. Elixir gives the team power and control over a system that manages thousands of connections and integrations between freight companies and their shippers.
