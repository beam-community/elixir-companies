GroupFlow is an all-in-one platform for running membership clubs and social groups: membership and dues, events and ticketing, online payments, announcements and email, member chat and forums, and each group's public website.

Its backend is written in Elixir with Phoenix and Ash, and serves the web and mobile apps through a GraphQL API. Background jobs such as renewals, reminders and email sends run in Elixir as well.
